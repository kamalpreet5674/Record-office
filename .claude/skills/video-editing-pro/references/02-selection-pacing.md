# Clip selection and pacing

## Shot scoring (0–5 each, keep ≥ 22/30; emotion overrides)
| Criterion | 5 | 0 |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | subject/eyes sharp | missed focus |
| Exposure | skin in range, highlights held | clipped face |
| Stability | locked or motivated motion | unmotivated shake/warp |
| Composition | clear subject, headroom, no limb cuts at joints | cluttered |
| Eye-line / direction | matches neighbours, 180° line kept | breaks direction |
| Emotional value | genuine peak moment | flat |
| **Authenticity (documentary)** | real, verified, right context | unverified / wrong event |

A slightly soft shot with the true emotional peak beats a perfect shot with none.

## Coverage
Establishing/wide (where) · medium (body language) · close-up (emotion) · insert/ECU (proof: document line, number, hands) · B-roll (show the claim, hide cuts) · cutaway/reaction · archival/photo (evidence: check source + context).

## Choosing takes
Performance/truth first → continuity (props, eye-line, light) → energy curve rising to payoff → clarity of the line → coverage logic (wide → closer as tension rises, widen for release).

## Pacing by content
| Content | Avg shot | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Talking head | 3–6 s with punch-ins | jump cuts 110–120%, B-roll on every claim, face never under a card |
| Tutorial/screen | 4–10 s | zoom to the click area; never cut mid-action users copy |
| Documentary/explainer (Shorts) | 1–3 s, new beat every 2–4 s | real photos/docs + Ken Burns, maps, one diagram that grows |
| Documentary (long) | 4–8 s | chapter cards, archival push-ins, breathing room |
| Cinematic/brand | 3–8 s | match cuts, L/J, speed ramps |
| Ad 6–30 s | 0.8–2 s | hook 1–2 s, product by 3 s, end card |
| Music video | beat/half-beat | vary every 8 bars |

Explainers: ground in a person/situation and a question; one concrete example worked start to finish; reveal each part when the voice names it (~19 spoken words per scene); end on the one idea in one line. Tells: bullet slides, icons in circles, three cards in a row, the whole diagram at once, narrator repeating on-screen text word for word.
Visual change cadence: dead stretch > 3 s with nothing changing is a warning (`check` flags `dead_stretch`).
