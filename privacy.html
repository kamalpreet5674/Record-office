Privacy Policy

App: Records Office Uploader
Last updated: 22 November 2026

What the app does

Records Office Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of The Records Office YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own videos and reads the channel's own public video list and statistics using the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to the owner's YouTube channel data (youtube.readonly), used to avoid repeating topics.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored privately by the owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

For any question, contact the channel owner through The Records Office YouTube channel.
