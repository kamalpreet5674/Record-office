# Growth plan: 1,000 subscribers by 30 December 2026

Start (23 Nov 2026): **12 subscribers**, 6,417 views and 48% average viewed in the last 28 days.
Target: **1,000 by 30 Dec**, about 988 more in 37 days, roughly 27 a day.
Honest note: small explainer channels usually get about 1 to 3 subscribers per 1,000 Shorts views,
so we need a few hundred thousand views, which means a few Shorts must break out. Steady work alone
will not reach it; the plan is to make every Short a strong attempt and learn fast from the numbers.

## First audit, 23 Nov 2026 (data/audits/2026-11-23.md)
- Retention decides views: every Short at about 60% average viewed got 670 to 1,460 views; every Short
  under 50% stayed under 260. Target 65%+.
- Winners were timely shocks with a concrete number or event (US midterms "7 Seats", "358 Billion Litre",
  Microsoft outage, Malacca). Losers: English titles, "Explained"/"samjhiye" framing, 100+ second length.
- Subscribers per Short are still tiny (0 to 3), so growth must come from more Shorts reaching 1,000+ views.
- Posting time: 15:00-18:00 Dubai did best, so the evening slot moved to 17:30 (19:00 IST).

## What the pipeline does for it (automatic)
1. **2 Shorts every day, no gaps.** Consistency feeds the Shorts algorithm.
2. **Retention first:** 60 to 70 s, hook in 2 s, a new visual every 2 to 4 s, no slow middle.
   Goal: 65%+ average viewed (our top Shorts were at about 60%).
3. **Timely topics with a concrete number or event** (our best: "7 Seats", "358 Billion Litre",
   Microsoft outage). At least one of the two daily Shorts rides that day's news.
4. **Titles:** concrete and curious, a place, number or India angle; never "Explained".
5. **Learn every 3 days** (Phase C) and double down on what wins: if one topic type wins twice, make more.
6. **Owner feedback becomes rules the same day** (see "Learning loop" in CLAUDE.md).

## Tests (owner said YES to all 5 on 23 Nov 2026; rules in CLAUDE.md "Growth tests")
- **Soft follow line:** the style says never "like/subscribe". Test a story-style last line instead,
  for example "Aise hi records roz kholte hain." on half the Shorts for 1 week.
- **Pinned comment with a question** under every Short (owner pins it in Studio, 10 seconds).
- **One long video a week** made from the best Short's topic (long videos turn viewers into subscribers
  much better; needs more of the owner's time).
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour** (owner, from the phone).
- **Small paid promotion** of the best Short with Google Ads (YouTube). It can add views and subscribers,
  but paid subscribers watch less, so only try a small budget on a Short that already holds 65%+.
