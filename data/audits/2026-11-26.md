## Deep dive: why the views stop (YouTube Analytics, 1 Oct to 26 Nov)

### Where views come from
| source | views | share |
|---|---|---|
| Shorts feed | 6,403 | 88% |
| YouTube search | 416 | 6% |
| Suggested videos | 168 | 2% |
| Subscribers / browse | 101 | 1% |
Search, browse and subscribers give almost nothing yet. **Our reach depends on the Shorts feed alone**, and the feed
decides with two numbers: how many viewers stay past the first second(s), and how much of the Short they watch.

### The pattern: one-day burst, then nothing
Daily views jump on the upload day (5 Nov 1,387 · 9 Nov 1,169 · 19 Nov 1,250) and fall to 10 to 150 the next day.
The feed shows each Short to a test group of about 500 to 1,500 people, the numbers are not good enough for the
next, bigger group, and it stops. No Short has broken past ~1,500 views.

### Leak 1 (biggest): the first seconds
- **Stayed % (engaged views / views) is 52 to 62% on every Short, even the winners.** About 4 in 10 people swipe
  away before the Short really starts. Shorts that break out usually keep 70%+.
- YouTube's own comparison with similar-length videos (relative retention, 0.5 = average) is **below average at
  the 5 to 10% mark on every single Short**: Trump 0.49 · MS outage 0.48 · Iran 0.62 · Nvidia 0.33 · GTA 6 0.17 ·
  AI bill 0.14. Our middles and endings are average or better (0.6 to 0.85); **the opening is where we lose.**

### Leak 2: a slow patch around seconds 7 to 20
Audience kept (1.0 = everyone): GTA 6 falls 0.89 → 0.41 between 10% and 25% of the video; AI bill 0.66 → 0.44;
Nvidia 0.74 → 0.57. This is where scripts go to background ("Shuru se samajhiye", context, a second number before
the first one has landed). The winners lose less here (Iran 1.00 → 0.79).

### Leak 3: too few lottery tickets
Target is 2 Shorts a day (14 a week). In the last 7 days the channel got 3 (GTA 6, Iran, RBI-Tata).
The routines are not switched on yet (data/HANDOFF.md). Each Short is one test in the feed; with ~1 in 4 Shorts
reaching 1,000+ views, 3 a week cannot reach 1,000 subscribers.

### Leak 4: length and packaging
- Most Shorts were 90 to 130 s. Same 60% viewed on a 130 s Short is fine, but long Shorts lose more people in the
  middle. Iran (91 s) did 68%, our best; the new 60 to 70 s rule should push this higher. Today's RBI-Tata Short is
  100 s again (not made by the pipeline).
- Titles mix English, Roman Hinglish and Devanagari; the three English titles and the two English long videos got
  3 to 66 views. Titles carry 2 to 4 hashtags and get cut off.
- Comments are almost zero (0 on all top 5), subscribers per 1,000 views ≈ 1.8. Nothing asks the viewer to react.

### What is working (keep it)
- Big global news **that day** + one concrete number + India stake (7 Seats, 358 billion litre, MS outage, Hormuz):
  all four winners are this type. Late or company-only finance topics (Jio IPO, Binance, Nvidia, Rupaya, Russian oil
  posted late) all stayed under 200.
- Audience is clear: 89% male, 25 to 44 the core, India 65% (+ Nepal, Pakistan, Bangladesh 13%). Hinglish is right.
- Middle and end retention are fine; people who get past the hook stay.

### GTA 6 (the most edited Short so far)
37% viewed, 53% stayed, relative 0.16 to 0.17 in the opening. Heavy effects did not save it: the premise
(game sales vs Indian box office) is not what our news/power audience came for, and the two-number comparison came
too early. One video only, so a HYPOTHESIS, not a rule: **topic and hook beat editing**.

## Fixes (in order of impact)
1. **Post 2 Shorts every day, no gaps.** Switch on the routines (ROUTINE_PROMPT.md) in claude.ai/code today.
   This alone roughly 4x the number of feed tests per week.
2. **Rebuild the first 2 seconds (fixes leak 1):**
   - Frame 1 is already the most striking visual of the story (ship vs navy, the map, the money), moving; no logo,
     no fade-in, no dark card.
   - A big on-screen hook text (4 to 6 words, with the number) visible from frame 1, before the voice finishes.
   - Voice starts at 0.0 s. Line 1 carries the conflict and the number in under 8 words. "Socho..." is fine, but
     the first pause comes after the image is clear, not before.
   - Check before upload: watch the first 3 s muted; if it is not clear what the story is, redo it.
3. **No background in seconds 5 to 20 (fixes leak 2):** after the hook, go straight to the stake ("aur iska bill
   Bharat bharega") and keep the explanation for after the first reveal. One number at a time; cut lines like
   "Shuru se samajhiye".
4. **Topic filter:** at least one Short a day must be that day's big news, scripted the same morning and live
   within 12 to 24 h. Finance only as a power/India story with a shock number, never plain company news or fandom.
5. **60 to 70 s, hard limit** (already in CLAUDE.md); apply it to hand-made Shorts too.
6. **Titles:** Roman Hinglish only, under 55 characters, the number + the place/actor, at most 2 hashtags
   (#Shorts + one). No English titles, no Devanagari mix, no "Explained/samjhiye". No more English long videos.
7. **Loop ending:** last line flows into the first so replays push average viewed above 70%.
8. **Comments and subs:** pinned question comment on every Short, reply to every comment in the first hour,
   follow-line test continues to 30 Nov.
9. **Measure:** this audit now shows "stayed %" per Short. Targets: stayed 70%+, average viewed 70%+.
