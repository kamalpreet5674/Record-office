## What the numbers suggest
- HYPOTHESIS: Retention decides a Short. All top 4 Shorts held about 60% viewed; every Short under
  47% stayed under 260 views. The first 3 seconds and no slow middle matter most. Our new 60 to 70 s
  length should push % viewed higher than the old 95 to 130 s Shorts.
- HYPOTHESIS: Big news that week + a concrete number or a concrete event wins (US midterms "7 Seats",
  Microsoft outage, "358 Billion Litre"). Timely Current Affairs and Tech-shock topics beat evergreen
  explainers.
- HYPOTHESIS: "Explainer" framing loses ("100 second mein samjhiye", "Explained", "Why Reliance is..."):
  lead with the shock or the mystery, not with "let me explain".
- HYPOTHESIS: Hinglish titles beat English titles. All three English titles did badly.
- HYPOTHESIS: India-China / India-angle geopolitics builds returning viewers (Malacca 28, Undersea
  cables 27, AI water 32). Good for subscribers even when views are mid.
- HYPOTHESIS: Pure finance-company news without a hook (Jio IPO, Nvidia, Binance, Rupaya) does poorly
  for us so far. Finance works when it becomes a world/India power story or a shocking number.
- A Short with high retention but almost no views (Russian Oil 100% Tariff, 12 views) means the feed
  never tested it, probably a stale topic by upload time. Upload Current Affairs the same day.

## Topic priority for now
1. Current Affairs / Geopolitics that is big news this week, with an India angle and a number.
2. Tech shock with a big number (AI, outages, cables, data centres).
3. Finance only as a power/India story with a shocking figure, not company news.
4. History/Geography with a "record" or surprising fact (Mawsynram-type did OK retention, low reach).
