# My script style: The Records Office Shorts

Claude reads this before every script. This style beats the general style advice in the
video-scripter skill, but never the fact rules in CLAUDE.md.

## The shape in one line
Start with a question or a paradox, build suspense in the middle, land one "asli twist", and end
with a question for the viewer or a line that stings. The payoff answers the hook.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
- **Never start with a number or a date.** Number-first hooks failed in the channel audit.
- Use a paradox, a direct question, or a "Socho..." scene followed by two stacked questions.
- Good examples:
  - "Jin scientists ne atom bomb banaya… unhi ne duniya ko ek ghadi di." (Doomsday)
  - "Agar India rupaye mein tel khareed sakta hai, toh duniya ko dollar ki zaroorat hi kyun hai?" (Dollar)
  - "Socho… duniya ka sabse taakatwar desh… aur sirf paanch din mein uska email ruka, aasmaan ruka, aur bank atak gaye." then "Toh phir America ko roka kisne? Aur kya yahi India mein bhi ho sakta hai?" (US outages)
  - "Ek insaan ko smart banane mein bees saal ka khaana lagta hai." (AI bijli, my own hook)
- Rejected: a flat figure hook like "Lagbhag saath hazaar crore rupaye ki chori" ("bekar"). Ask for suspense instead.

## Story structure
question → context → escalating events → twist → payoff that answers the hook.
1. **Plant the question:** "Par Binance ka apna system... chor ke liye kaam kaise karne laga?"
2. **Escalate:** events one after another, with lines like "Ye toh bas shuruaat thi."
3. **Number ladder:** "Pehle 37 crore. Phir 102. Aur ab... 184."
4. **Twist:** "Ab asli twist suno." / "Lekin ab asli jhatka suniye." The twist is often the **India angle** (UPI outage, Andaman-Nicobar, India–Pakistan, Indian banks).
5. **Payoff:** the hook's question comes back at the end: "Toh duniya ko dollar ki zaroorat kyun hai?"
6. It must be a **story**, not an explainer. An explainer-style draft with no story was rejected ("maza nahi aaya").

## Language and tone
- Roman Hinglish, spoken style, talking straight to the viewer ("aap", "socho"). Dramatic but not filmy.
- My fillers: "Socho…", "Aakhir kaise?", "Nateeja?", "Kyun?", "Ab sawaal…", "Aur haan", "Matlab", "Sawaal bas itna hai…"
- Pauses with "…": "Aur phir… woh hua jo pehle kabhi nahi hua."
- Put the viewer in the story: "Aapki free delivery? Unka nuksaan." / "Aap Maggi lene aaye the... chips, cold drink aur ice cream bhi leke gaye."
- Explain technical things in one simple line: "Ye wo engineer hote hain, jo exchange ke servers chalate hain."
- **Name who did what.** "America aur Europe ne Russia ke lagbhag 300 billion dollar ke reserves freeze kar diye." Never vague words like "West". Cause and effect step by step.

## Pacing, length, ending
- One line, one idea. Most lines 5 to 15 words: "Same rider. Same store. Zyada paisa."
- Question-answer beats: "Nateeja? 12,000 se zyada flights cancel."
- Voiceover runs at **1.10x**. For a 60 to 70 second Short that is about **180 to 200 spoken words**
  (Doomsday Clock: 191 words ≈ 68–72 sec).
- Ending: never "like/subscribe". Either a question to the viewer or a sharp closing line:
  - "Sawaal bas itna hai… is baar ghumayega kaun?"
  - "Ab aapke paise se ladi jaayegi."
  - "Aapko kya lagta hai... AI boom hai, ya bubble?"
  - "Ab sochiye — aapke office ka laptop... kis-kis darwaze tak jaata hai?"

## Influences, and where I am different
- Nitish Rajput (stacked questions, teasers, fillers), Dhruv Rathee (fact-based with sources),
  Think School (business breakdowns), Ankur Warikoo (connect money to the viewer's own life).
- Different from them:
  - An India angle in every global story.
  - Sources go in the description, **not in the voiceover**. Don't say "Reuters ke mutabik".
  - No opinions, ask questions instead: "Nvidia Cisco banega ya nahi, abhi koi nahi jaanta."
  - No claims about real people without proof.
  - Cut cringe dramatic lines (see the list below).

## Niches and angles
- Niches: History, Finance, Civics / Geopolitics, Geography, Technology, Current Affairs.
- Topics: trending or growing, for Indian viewers, not done on the channel before.
- Favourite angles: money and power (dollar, SWIFT, Nvidia), hidden systems (undersea cables, dark stores, routers), and the impact on India.

## Standing rules
1. Never start the hook with a number or date. Question in the first 3 seconds, suspense in the middle, payoff at the end.
2. Copyright-safe material only: no strikes, Content ID claims or monetization problems.
3. No AI faces of real public figures. No logos and no news clips.
4. Every fact verified with a source (Gate 2).
5. Sources go in the description, not the voiceover.
6. No unproven claims about real people.
7. When fixing facts, keep my wording and style.
8. Say clearly who did what. Cause and effect step by step.
9. If exact footage isn't available, build a custom visual from related clips and maps. Never leave a gap.

### Example 1: AI Kitni Bijli Kha Raha Hai?

Ek insaan ko smart banane mein bees saal ka khaana lagta hai.
Lekin AI ko smart banane ki daud mein… bijli kitni lagegi?
February 2026. New Delhi.
AI Impact Summit mein OpenAI ke CEO Sam Altman se AI ki energy par sawaal hota hai.
Altman kehte hain, insaan ko taiyaar karne mein bhi toh energy lagti hai.
Lekin woh ye bhi maante hain:
AI ki kul energy khapat par chinta jaayaz hai.
Kyunki ek sawaal ka jawaab dena…
aur poori duniya ke sawaalon ka jawaab dena…
dono ka bijli ka hisaab alag hai.
America mein OpenAI ka Stargate project dekhiye.
Epoch AI ke mutabik, saat sites ki planned capacity 2029 tak nau gigawatt se zyada ho sakti hai.
Texas ke Abilene mein toh bijli ka ek hissa site par hi gas jalaakar banaya ja raha hai.
Ireland mein 2025 mein desh ki metered bijli ka 23 pratishat data centres ne liya.
Aur ab isi daud mein Bharat bhi hai.
OpenAI aur Tata ki partnership: 100 megawatt se shuru, aage ek gigawatt tak.
Visakhapatnam mein Google ne ek gigawatt ke AI hub ka elaan kiya.
Par The Guardian ke mutabik, clearance ke kaagazon mein likha hai… 2.51 gigawatt.
Google ise gigawatt-scale hi kehta hai.
Shehar mein virodh ke ek banner par likha tha:
"We cannot drink data."
Google ka kehna hai ki air cooling ki wajah se peene ke paani par asar nahi padega.
Sochiye, jaise grid par ek naya shehar bas raha ho.
Ek aisa shehar… jo kabhi sota nahi.
Uski bijli ki keemat kaun chukayega?
Kyunki AI ka jawaab toh screen par aata hai…
Lekin use chalaane ki keemat, isi duniya mein chukaani padti hai.

### Example 2: Binance Hack

30 October, India mein raat ke ek bajkar paintis minute ho rahe the, aur duniya ke sabse bade crypto exchange, Binance, ke wallets se paisa nikalna shuru hua.
Bees minute mein zyaadatar rakam bahar thi.
Kul chori — kareeb saath hazaar crore rupaye ki hui, par sabse ajeeb baat ye hai ki...
Har transaction par... Binance ke apne wallets ka signature tha.
Ye crypto ke itihaas mein ab tak ki sabse badi chori hai.
Par Binance ka apna system... chor ke liye kaam kaise karne laga?
Binance ki shuruaati jaanch ek DevOps engineer ke laptop tak pahunchi.
Ye wo engineer hote hain, jo exchange ke servers chalate hain.
Isliye unke laptop se andar ke systems tak raasta hota hai.
Binance ke mutabik, hacker isi raaste se withdrawal bhejne wale system tak pahuncha.
Par laptop hack kaise hua... ye Binance ne abhi nahi bataya.
Binance ke co-CEO Richard Teng ke mutabik, ye ek click ka kaam nahi tha.
Hacker ne Ethereum ke ek naye feature, EIP-7702, ka fayda uthaya.
Isse wallet ka control hacker ke code ko mila, aur normal approval checks bypass ho gaye.
Unke mutabik, ek self-destruct code ne hacker ke nishaan bhi mita diye.
Baad mein breach ke aur raaste bhi mile.
Udhar, Tether aur Circle jaisi companies ne kareeb teen billion dollar freeze kar diye.
Lekin freeze ka matlab... paisa wapas milna nahi hota.
Phir FBI ne naam liya — North Korea.
Hacking unit — TraderTraitor, jo Lazarus network ka hissa hai.
Ye network engineers ko nakli job offer bhejkar, malware dene ke liye badnaam hai.
Is laptop ke saath bhi yahi hua ya nahi... abhi confirm nahi.
Aur North Korea ne na maana hai, na inkaar kiya.
Binance ka vaada hai, nuksaan users nahi, company uthayegi.
Aur kareeb pachaanve percent bhar bhi diya.
Par is chori ka asli sabak paisa nahi hai.
Hacker ne taala nahi toda...
usne wo system pakda, jo khud taala kholta hai.
Aur us system tak ka darwaza... shayad sirf ek laptop tha.
Ab sochiye — aapke office ka laptop... kis-kis darwaze tak jaata hai?

### Example 3: Dollar Se Bhaagne Ka Jaal

Agar India rupaye mein tel khareed sakta hai, toh duniya ko dollar ki zaroorat hi kyun hai?
2023 mein UAE se ye hua bhi. Lagbhag das lakh barrel, bina dollar ke.
Lekin yahi raasta, Russia ke liye ek badi musibat ban gaya.
2022 mein Ukraine war ke baad, America aur Europe ne Russia ke lagbhag 300 billion dollar ke reserves freeze kar diye.
Tab Russia ne doosre deshon ke saath, dollar ki jagah unki apni currency mein trade badhaya.
Russia ka daawa hai, China ke saath 99.1 percent payment ab rouble aur yuan mein hota hai.
Aur India ke saath, rupaye mein.
Yahin se problem shuru hui.
2024-25 mein India ne Russia se 63.8 billion dollar ka saamaan khareeda, zyada tar tel.
Aur Russia ne India se khareeda sirf 4.9 billion ka.
Matlab jitne rupaye Russia ko milte, utne wo India mein kharch hi nahi kar pata.
Aur India ke bahar, rupaya zyada chalta nahi.
Russia ke Foreign Minister ne khud maana, Indian banks mein unke arbon rupaye phanse hain.
Isliye Russian tel bechne wale, rupaye ki jagah dirham aur yuan maangne lage.
Par ek cheez ab bhi nahi badli.
Payment yuan mein, lekin tel ka daam? Ab bhi dollar mein.
Kyunki Russian tel par Western deshon ki price limit, dollar mein lagti hai.
Jis dollar se Russia bachna chahta tha, wahi har deal mein baitha tha.
Toh duniya ko dollar ki zaroorat kyun hai?
Kyunki aaj bhi currency badalne ke 89 percent saudon mein dollar shaamil hai.
Aur duniya ke central banks apni bachat ka lagbhag 57 percent dollar mein rakhte hain. Yuan mein sirf 2.
Local currency mein trade ho sakta hai.
Par dollar ki jagah lena? Abhi bahut door hai.

### Example 4: Quick Commerce Burn War

Jab bhi aap 10 minute mein grocery mangwate ho... peeche ek jung chal rahi hoti hai.
Aisi jung jisme teen companies ek dusre ko kangal karne pe tuli hain.
Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart — teeno saalon se ek hi kaam kar rahi thi... paisa jalana.
Aapki free delivery? Unka nuksaan.
Aapka discount? Aur bada nuksaan.
Zepto ko har order pe 60 rupaye ka ghaata tha... Instamart ko 76.
Aur Zepto ne toh ek saal mein 5,900 crore phoonk diye.
Ab aap sochoge... ye pagal hain kya?
Nahi. Ye ek shart hai.
Shart ye — ki jo sabse zyada der tak paisa jala sakta hai... market uska.
Baaki sab? Game over.
Aur 2026 mein is shart ka pehla winner saamne aa gaya.
Blinkit.
Wahi Blinkit jo khud har quarter 150 crore se zyada jala raha tha... usne achanak profit kamana shuru kar diya.
Pehle 37 crore. Phir 102. Aur ab... 184.
Aakhir kaise?
Kyunki Blinkit speed ka game khel hi nahi raha tha. Wo density ka game khel raha tha.
Har mohalle mein ek dark store — wo godaam jisme aap kabhi ghus nahi sakte.
Jitne zyada stores... utne paas ke orders... utni sasti har delivery.
Aur ek chalaki — bada basket.
Aap Maggi lene aaye the... chips, cold drink aur ice cream bhi leke gaye.
Blinkit ka average order 525 rupaye ka hai... Zepto ka sirf 387.
Same rider. Same store. Zyada paisa.
Yahi wo maths hai jo Zepto aaj tak nahi bana paayi.
Aur sabse mazedaar baat?
Jis Zepto ne 5,900 crore jalaye... wo ab stock market se 8,000 crore aur maangne jaa rahi hai.
Matlab ab tak ye jung investors ke paise se ladi jaa rahi thi...
Ab aapke paise se ladi jaayegi.

### Example 5: Undersea Internet Cables

Duniya ki poori economy kuch garden-hose jitni patli taaron par latki hai — samundar ke neeche, total andhere mein.
Aur ye taarein katna shuru ho chuki hain.
Kyunki jo internet aap abhi use kar rahe hain, wo satellites se nahi aata.
Duniya ka lagbhag ninety-nine percent international data samundar ke neeche bichhi cables se guzarta hai.
Kareeb chhe sau active cables, chaudah lakh kilometre lambi.
Inhi taaron se roz lagbhag das trillion dollar ke financial transactions guzarte hain.
Ab socho — koi inhe jaan-boojh kar kaate to kya ho?
Ye sawaal hypothetical nahi hai.
November 2024 — Baltic Sea mein do din ke andar do cables kat gayin.
Ek Lithuania aur Sweden ke beech, doosri Finland aur Germany ke beech.
Germany ke defense minister ne kaha — koi nahi maanta ki ye accident tha.
September 2025 — Red Sea mein cables kati, aur India, Pakistan, UAE samet kareeb das deshon ka internet slow pad gaya.
Microsoft ko warning deni padi ki cloud services late chalengi.
Ab imagine karo ek full-scale conflict, jahan ye cables target banein.
Banking freeze, cloud down, stock markets andhere mein, military communications cut.
Aur satellites? Wo ek percent se bhi kam sambhaal paate hain.
Lekin sabse dangerous baat ab aati hai.
Poori duniya mein cable ships sirf saath ke kareeb hain — aur dedicated repair ships bees se bhi kam.
Ek cable theek karne mein hafte lag jaate hain.
2006 mein Taiwan ke paas ek earthquake ne aath cable systems tod diye — aur kai deshon ka internet raat-o-raat beth gaya.
Wo sirf ek accident tha.
Agli badi jung ka pehla hamla shayad dikhai hi na de.
Na dhamaka, na siren — bas screens ek-ek karke band hongi.
Aur tab duniya ko samajh aayega ki wo kitni patli taar par latki thi.

### Example 6: Malacca Dilemma: China ki Pipeline Race

Naqshe par yeh bas ek patli si lakeer hai — Malaysia aur Indonesia ke beech.
Lekin 2003 mein China ke President Hu Jintao ne isi lakeer ko apne desh ki sabse badi kamzori bataya tha.
Naam diya — "Malacca Dilemma".
Aur aaj China is ek raste se bachne ke liye pahadon ke beech arabon dollar ki pipelines bichha raha hai.
Yeh hai Strait of Malacca — sabse patli jagah par sirf dhai kilometer chauda.
Har roz duniya ka takreeban 23 million barrel oil yahin se guzarta hai.
Aur China ka lagbhag 80 percent imported oil isi raste se aata hai.
Problem yeh hai ki is raste ke entrance par India ke Andaman-Nicobar islands hain — Indian Navy ke bases ke saath.
Matlab tension ke waqt koi bhi taqatwar navy is raste ko rok sakti hai... aur China ki economy thap.
Isliye China ne backup routes banane shuru kiye.
Pehla — Myanmar pipeline.
771 kilometer lambi yeh pipeline Kyaukphyu port se pahadon ke beech hoti hui seedha China pahunchti hai.
Kharcha — lagbhag dhai billion dollars; 2017 se isme oil beh raha hai.
Doosra — Pakistan ka Gwadar port, jahan se pipeline banane ka plan hai.
Teesra — Russia aur Kazakhstan se zameen ke raaste alag pipelines.
Aur saath mein 1.4 billion barrels ka emergency oil stockpile.
Lekin ab asli jhatka suniye.
Myanmar wali pipeline China ki oil imports ka sirf paanch percent handle kar sakti hai.
Saari pipelines milkar bhi samundar wale raste ki jagah nahi le sakti.
Isliye bees saal aur arabon dollar ke baad bhi... Malacca Dilemma aaj tak solve nahi hua.
Jab tak China ka oil samundar se aata hai — yeh patli si lakeer hi uski sabse badi tension rahegi.

### Example 7: Doomsday Clock: 85 Second

Nahi, ye 13 November wali afwaah nahi hai.

Wajah? Badi taakaton ka takraav… aur haan, May 2025 ka India–Pakistan takraav bhi.
